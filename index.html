关于我

I am a Software Engineering undergraduate at Pujiang Institute, Nanjing Tech University. My work sits at the intersection of deep learning and software engineering, with computer vision as the primary research direction.

I approach research code as software that should remain understandable, reproducible and usable. I prefer working end-to-end systems, explicit module boundaries and evidence-driven complexity over one-off experimental code.
